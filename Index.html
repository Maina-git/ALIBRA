Questions

Where do you deliver?+−

We deliver across Kenya. Same-day delivery is available within Nairobi, and express delivery takes 24–48 hours for all other towns and counties.

How can I pay?+−

We accept Lipa na M-Pesa (Till / Paybill) as well as Visa and Mastercard debit or credit cards.

Are the items authentic?+−

Yes, 100%. Every piece of clothing and pair of footwear is individually inspected for material quality, stitching, and finish before dispatch.

Can I exchange an item?+−

Yes! If you need a size exchange or different fit, notify us via WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn and in original condition.
